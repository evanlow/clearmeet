Guidance for Outcome-Driven Objectives

  • Business Issue: What specific problem needs solving? Be concrete.
  • Objective: What do you want to achieve? Use action verbs (align, decide, approve, resolve).
  • Expected Output: What tangible result should emerge? (decision, plan, approval, assignment)

Example: Instead of "Discuss project status," write "Review project blockers and assign clear owners to resolve delays"
